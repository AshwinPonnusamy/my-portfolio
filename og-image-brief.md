# Open Graph (OG) Image Brief

The OG image is what appears when your site is shared on LinkedIn, WhatsApp, and Twitter. It must look professional and provide immediate context.

## Technical Specifications
- **Dimensions**: 1200 × 630 pixels
- **Aspect Ratio**: 1.91:1
- **File Format**: JPEG or PNG (highly optimized, < 200KB)
- **Path**: `/public/og-image.jpg`

## Visual Elements

### 1. Background
- Use a dark, sleek gradient (e.g., #0f172a to #1e293b) to match your site's dark mode.
- Subtle glassmorphic patterns or floating code symbols (React logo, `</>`) to hint at the tech stack.

### 2. Typography
- **Primary Text**: "ASHWIN" (Bold, Large, Sans-serif like Inter or Outfit).
- **Secondary Text**: "Frontend Developer & UI Designer" (Medium weight, accent color like #3b82f6).
- **CTA/URL**: "aboutashwin.netlify.app" (Small, bottom corner).

### 3. Layout
- **Left Side**: Large text branding (Name & Role).
- **Right Side**: High-quality portrait photo of yourself OR a high-fidelity screenshot of your best project. Use a rounded frame or a glassmorphic card container.
- **Center Focus**: Ensure the most important text is within the "safe zone" (central 1000px) so it isn't cropped on mobile previews.

## Branding Goal
When a recruiter sees this image on LinkedIn, it should communicate:
1. **Name Recognition**: They know who you are immediately.
2. **Technical Depth**: They see your core skills (React, UI/UX).
3. **Aesthetic Quality**: The design of the image reflects the quality of your work.

## Implementation Tip
You can use tools like **Canva**, **Figma**, or even a screenshot of your hero section (with extra padding) to create this. Once created, save it as `og-image.jpg` in your `public/` folder.
