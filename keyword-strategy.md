### Meta Tags
Ensure the `seo-head-tags.html` provided are used. They contain the optimal primary keywords in the title and description.

## 4. Branding for "[Ashwin's Full Name] Developer"
To rank #1 for your name:
1. **Name in Title**: Always keep "Ashwin" at the start of your page title.
2. **Alt Text**: Use your full name in the `alt` tag of your profile image (e.g., `alt="Ashwin - Frontend Developer Portrait"`).
3. **Backlinks**: Ensure your social profiles (GitHub, LinkedIn) link back to `https://aboutashwin.netlify.app/`.

## 5. GitHub & LinkedIn Optimization
*   **LinkedIn**: Update your headline to match your portfolio title: "Frontend Developer | React | UI/UX Designer". Link your portfolio in the "Featured" section.
*   **GitHub**: Add your portfolio URL to your GitHub profile bio. Use relevant tags like `#react`, `#tailwindcss`, `#frontend` in your top repositories.
*   **Consistency**: Use the same professional profile picture across all platforms to build brand recognition for search engines.
